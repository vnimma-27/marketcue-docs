TickerCue — Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 22, 2026 · Last updated: May 22, 2026

Short version: TickerCue has no user accounts, no logins, no ads, and no third-party analytics or trackers. To deliver your market briefs at the times you choose — even when the app is closed — TickerCue sends your notification token, your watchlist of ticker symbols, and your schedule to TickerCue's own server. We never collect your name, email, phone number, location, or any payment or financial-account information.

Who we are

TickerCue is published by Compass Labs, an independent app publisher. For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at contact.market.cue@gmail.com.

What TickerCue does NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • We do not collect your device's geographic location (GPS or IP-based).
  • We do not require an account or sign-in.
  • We do not collect any payment, banking, brokerage, or financial-account information.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

What TickerCue stores on your device

The app stores the following locally on your phone using Android's standard AsyncStorage mechanism. This data is removed when you uninstall the app:

  • Your watchlist of stock ticker symbols (e.g., "SPY", "AAPL").
  • Your chosen brief times (e.g., 9:15 AM, 12:30 PM).
  • Settings flags: weekdays-only toggle, mute / pause state, onboarding-complete flag.
  • An optional on-device log of recently delivered briefs (the "Brief History" debug feature), if you enable it.

What TickerCue sends to its server

So that briefs can be delivered on schedule even when the app is closed, TickerCue operates a small server (the "TickerCue server", hosted on Cloudflare Workers). When you open the app or change your settings, the app sends the following to that server over an encrypted HTTPS connection:

  • A push notification token issued to your app install by Firebase Cloud Messaging. This identifies your app installation so the server can deliver a notification to it. It is not tied to your real-world identity.
  • Your watchlist of ticker symbols.
  • Your brief schedule (the times you chose), the weekdays-only toggle, mute / pause state, your move-percentage threshold, and your device's time zone.

The server uses this only to fetch current market prices and send your scheduled brief notifications. The server also keeps a short rolling log (up to the 100 most recent briefs) of what it delivered to your app install — the ticker percentage moves and timestamps — so that the in-app "Brief History" screen can show you what was sent. This log contains market data and timestamps only; it contains no personal identity information.

If your app install stops contacting the server for 60 days (for example, because you uninstalled the app), the server automatically deletes your subscription and its associated history. You can also delete this data at any time — see Data deletion below.

Third-party services

TickerCue relies on the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare Workers — hosts the TickerCue server described above. Cloudflare processes the data on our behalf as a service provider. Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — the delivery transport for push notifications. Google issues the push token and routes notifications to your device. Firebase privacy information.
  • Yahoo Finance public market-data endpoint (query1.finance.yahoo.com) — queried by the TickerCue server to obtain current stock prices. These requests contain only ticker symbols, not any information about you. Yahoo privacy policy.

We do not consider any of the above to be "selling" or "sharing" your personal data — they are service providers acting on our behalf to operate the app's core feature.

Permissions

TickerCue requests the following Android permission:

  • Post notifications — used solely to deliver the scheduled market briefs you configure inside the app at the times you choose.

Notifications

Scheduled briefs are normally delivered by the TickerCue server as push notifications, so that the prices shown are current as of the moment the brief fires. If the server cannot be reached, the app falls back to notifications scheduled locally on your device. Disabling notifications in your phone settings stops all briefs immediately.

Children

TickerCue is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has somehow shared personal information with the app, contact us and we will assist.

Data retention & deletion

Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. Data held by the TickerCue server (your push token, watchlist, schedule, and delivered-brief log) can be deleted on request and is also deleted automatically 60 days after your app install stops contacting the server. Full step-by-step instructions are on the TickerCue data deletion page.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change how the app handles data, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
