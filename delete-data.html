TickerCue — Data Deletion

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Summary: TickerCue (published by Compass Labs) has no user accounts. You can delete all data held about your app installation directly from the app, or it is deleted automatically once your install stops contacting our server. Details below.

What data exists

TickerCue stores two kinds of data:

  • On your device — your watchlist, brief schedule, settings, and an optional local brief-history log. This is removed entirely when you uninstall the app.
  • On the TickerCue server — your push notification token, your watchlist, your schedule, and a rolling log of up to the 100 most recently delivered briefs (ticker percentage moves and timestamps). This is what the steps below remove.

How to delete your data

Option 1 — Clear delivered-brief history from within the app

  1. Open TickerCue.
  2. Go to the Schedule tab, then open Brief History.
  3. Tap Clear history.

This deletes the server-side delivered-brief log for your device.

Option 2 — Remove your subscription entirely

To remove your push token, watchlist, and schedule from the server, turn off TickerCue notifications, then uninstall the app:

  1. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → TickerCue → Notifications and turn notifications off; or pause briefs inside the app.
  2. Uninstall TickerCue (long-press the app icon → Uninstall).

Once your install stops contacting the server, your subscription and all associated server-side data are automatically and permanently deleted after 60 days. Uninstalling also removes everything stored on the device immediately.

Option 3 — Email request

If you would like your server-side data deleted immediately rather than waiting for the automatic 60-day removal, email contact.market.cue@gmail.com from any address with the subject line "TickerCue data deletion". Because TickerCue has no accounts, please include enough detail for us to identify your install (for example, your watchlist symbols and brief times). We will delete the matching server record and confirm by reply, typically within 7 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your push notification token, watchlist, schedule, settings synced to the server, and your delivered-brief history log.
  • Kept: nothing that identifies you. TickerCue holds no name, email, account, payment, or location data, so there is nothing of that kind to retain.
